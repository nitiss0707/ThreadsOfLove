Other ways to give

Not ready to give online?

  • Donate items. New or gently used kids' clothing and school supplies. Drop-off & shipping details coming soon — sign up on our Get Involved page to be notified.
  • Host a drive. Organize a clothing or school-supply drive at your workplace, school, or church.
  • Corporate giving. Ask your employer about matching gifts or a company sponsorship.
  • Spread the word. Sometimes the most valuable gift is an introduction to someone who can help.
