Wanda's story

Every foundation starts somewhere. Ours started with a suitcase.

Photo of Wanda
coming soon

Wanda grew up in a family with very little in the Dominican Republic. Her grandmother had moved to the United States, and whenever she came back to visit, she brought a suitcase packed with used clothes, second-hand dolls and toys, and whatever living supplies she could carry.

To anyone else, it might have looked like hand-me-downs. To Wanda, it was everything. She remembers the joy of those visits vividly — because she had so little, and because someone had thought of her enough to carry those things across an ocean.

As an adult, Wanda looks back on those memories with gratitude — and with a simple wish: to give other children in need that same feeling. Threads of Love is how she's doing it.

How it happened

From one suitcase to a foundation.

Childhood in the Dominican Republic

Wanda grows up in an impoverished family, where clothing, supplies, and basic necessities are never guaranteed.

Her grandmother's visits

Her grandmother, living in the U.S., visits with a suitcase of used clothes, second-hand toys, and supplies — and it becomes one of the most meaningful parts of Wanda's childhood.

Looking back, as an adult

Wanda reflects on those visits with deep gratitude, and recognizes how much a small act of generosity can mean to a child who has very little.

Threads of Love is founded

Wanda starts Threads of Love to bring that same feeling — of being seen, and cared for — to more children and families across the Dominican Republic.

About the foundation

Just getting started — thank you for being early.

Threads of Love was founded in 2026 to serve children and families across the Dominican Republic. We're volunteer-run, community-rooted, and focused on three things: clothing, school supplies, and direct financial support.
