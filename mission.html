Our mission

What we believe every kid deserves.

Threads of Love exists so that no child in the Dominican Republic has to go without clothing, school supplies, or basic support — the same way one suitcase, once, meant everything to our founder.

Vision

A community where no child is left out because of what their family can't afford.

We want kids across the Dominican Republic to walk into school dressed, supplied, and ready — and we want their families to have somewhere to turn when things get hard.

Approach

Direct, personal, and dignity-first.

We're a young, volunteer-run foundation. That means low overhead, and it means every donation is handled the way Wanda's grandmother handled hers: personally, and with care for the person receiving it.

How we help

Three ways we show up for families.

Clothing drives & distribution

We collect new and gently used clothing sized for kids and teens, sort it by age and season, and distribute it directly to families and community partners across the DR.

School supply kits

Ahead of each school year, we assemble supply kits by grade level — notebooks, uniforms, backpacks, and basic tools — so kids start the year on equal footing.

Direct financial support

For families facing a sudden hardship — a medical bill, a missed rent payment, an emergency — we offer direct financial assistance, evaluated case by case.

The need, in numbers

Context for why this work matters.

According to UNICEF's 2025 report on the Dominican Republic, child monetary poverty measured about 31% in 2024, down from 36% the year before — real progress, but still nearly one in three kids. Multidimensional child poverty, which looks at housing, water, and school access rather than income alone, sat at 18%, and stays highest in rural and border communities. The World Food Programme notes poverty is also concentrated among households headed by women.

Sources: UNICEF Dominican Republic Annual Report 2025; World Food Programme country brief.

Why we focus on 3 things

Clothing, school supplies, and emergency funds are the three needs Wanda saw up close growing up — and the three a family can't easily plan around. They're specific enough to act on directly, and universal enough to matter everywhere we go.

What guides us

Our values

01

Dignity first

We give the way Wanda's grandmother gave — without judgment, and without making anyone feel small for needing help.

02

Direct impact

We're volunteer-run and lean on purpose, so more of every gift reaches a family instead of overhead.

03

Community-rooted

We work with local partners in the Dominican Republic who already know their communities, instead of parachuting in.

04

Gratitude, always

Wanda never forgot what a suitcase of hand-me-downs meant to her. That gratitude is the whole reason this foundation exists.
