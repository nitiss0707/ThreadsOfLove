A Dominican Republic foundation

It started with a suitcase of hand‑me‑downs.

When Wanda was a girl in the Dominican Republic, her grandmother would visit from the U.S. carrying a suitcase of used clothes, second‑hand toys, and whatever else she could fit. It was everything. Threads of Love exists to be that suitcase for more families — clothing, school supplies, and financial support for kids across the DR who are going without.

Why we exist

Growing up in the Dominican Republic can mean going without.

Nationally, roughly three in ten Dominican children are growing up in monetary poverty — and multidimensional poverty, lacking things like adequate housing, water, or school materials, runs even higher in rural and border communities. Those numbers are statistics. To a kid, it's a lot simpler: it's showing up to school without the right shoes, or a backpack, or supplies for a project everyone else already has.

Clothing

Seasonally appropriate clothing for kids who've outgrown what they have — or never had enough to begin with.

School Supplies

Backpacks, notebooks, and materials so a child can start the school year ready, not behind.

Financial Support

Direct assistance for families facing a hard month — rent, medical costs, or an emergency that won't wait.

Source: UNICEF Dominican Republic Annual Report 2025; World Food Programme.

Our founder

One suitcase changed how Wanda saw the world.

She grew up with very little. When her grandmother visited from the U.S. with hand-me-down clothes and second-hand toys, it meant the world — not because the items were new, but because someone had thought of her. Threads of Love is Wanda's way of being that someone for more families.
